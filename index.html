Who we are

Around the Block is a team of five Stevens students connecting Stevens students with part-time work in Hoboken.

Many students don't know which local businesses are hiring, and long applications are hard to fit around a class schedule. We bring open positions together in one place, with the hours and walking distance clear up front, and applications are sent from students' own Stevens email accounts.

For local businesses, it is a direct way to reach motivated Stevens students who live and study nearby. Questions or feedback?
